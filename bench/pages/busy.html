Live: the story that keeps updating

Updated now

The page keeps a ticker scrolling, a clock ticking and an animated banner running while you read. Analytics scripts wake up several times a second to record what you are doing. None of it is what you came for, and all of it runs for as long as the tab is on screen.

On the right, two advertisements load from another site. One draws an animation with JavaScript; the other plays a muted video on a loop.

Browsers can throttle some of this: small cross-site frames that you are not interacting with, animations nobody is looking at, and timers on pages that are hidden.
